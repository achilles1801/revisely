Help keep Revisely free.

Revisely has no ads and nothing to buy, and it’s the same for everyone. It’s made by one developer, and donations from the people who use it help keep it that way.

Any amount helps. You’ll pay on a secure checkout page.

Where it goes

What a donation pays for.

  • Sync

    The servers that keep your plan and history the same on every device.

  • The App Store

    Apple’s yearly developer membership, which keeps Revisely on iPhone.

  • Android

    Time to bring Revisely to Google Play.

  • Your ideas

    Fixes and new features, many of them suggested by people who use Revisely.

Good to know

Before you give.

  • Nothing to unlock. Every feature is free for everyone, and a donation doesn’t change what you get.
  • Not a charity. Revisely doesn’t collect zakat, and donations aren’t tax‑deductible.
  • Secure checkout. You pay on the payment provider’s page; Revisely never sees your card details.
  • Questions? Email support@revisely.app about anything to do with a donation.

Other ways to help

A rating on the App Store, or telling someone who’s memorizing, helps Revisely too.
