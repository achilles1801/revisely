How it works

Set it up once. Then just open the app.

  1. 1

    Mark what you’ve memorized

    By surah, juz or page. Add more as you memorize it, and the cycle grows with you.

  2. 2

    Choose how much to revise a day

    A number of pages, a juz or more, or a plan you build day by day, starting from any point.

  3. 3

    Revise today’s pages

    Tap Start revision and go through them in the mushaf, checking off each page as you finish it.

The mushaf

Revise on real pages, and hear them recited.

Every page of the Madani mushaf, laid out as it is in print. Press and hold a verse to read its translation or to play the recitation from there.

  • Saheeh International translation, verse by verse
  • Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi, Abdul Basit, Alafasy and more
  • A teaching recitation that pauses after each verse for you to repeat
Carry-over

Didn’t finish yesterday? Pick up where you left off.

If you stopped partway through yesterday’s pages, Home offers to add the ones you missed to today. Today keeps its usual size, and the rest of your plan moves back to make room.

Further behind? Move the whole plan back a day. The schedule never piles up.

Activity

See your month at a glance.

Each day you revise fills in on the calendar, darker the more you did. Tap a day to see what you covered and for how long, or switch to a week for the detail.

FAQ

Questions

Is Revisely free?

Yes. It’s free to download and use, and there are no ads.

Is there an Android version?

It’s coming to Google Play. For now, Revisely is on iPhone.

Which mushaf does it use?

The 604-page Madani mushaf, set in the King Fahd Complex script, with the same page layout as the printed copy many people memorize from.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your plan keeps moving, so missed pages don’t pile up. If you started yesterday’s revision but didn’t finish, Home offers to add the pages you missed to today, and you can always move the plan back a day.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google or email so your progress is saved and syncs between devices. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in Settings.
