How it works

Set it up once. Then just open the app.

  1. 1

    Mark what you’ve memorized

    By surah, juz or page. Add more as you memorize it, and the cycle grows with you.

  2. 2

    Choose how much to revise a day

    A number of pages, a juz or more, or a plan you build day by day, starting from any point.

  3. 3

    Revise today’s pages

    Tap Start revision and go through them in the mushaf, checking off each page as you finish it.

The mushaf

Revise on real pages, and hear them recited.

Every page of the Madani mushaf, laid out as it is in print. Press and hold a verse to read its translation, play the recitation from there, or copy it to share.

  • Saheeh International, or The Clear Quran® by Dr. Mustafa Khattab, verse by verse
  • Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi, Abdul Basit, Alafasy and more, including a teaching recitation
  • Change the speed, repeat each verse, or pause after it to recite it back
  • Keeps playing with the phone locked, with controls on the Lock Screen
Revision

One page at a time. Pick up where you left off.

Today’s pages open in the mushaf, one after another. Swipe past a page to check it off. With Smart Tracking on, you can also rate how well you know it.

Stopped partway yesterday? Home offers to add the pages you missed to today. Today keeps its usual size, and the rest of your plan moves back to make room. Been away? Jump to any surah or juz and carry on from there. The schedule never piles up.

Activity

See your month at a glance.

Each day you revise fills in on the calendar, darker the more you did. Tap a day to see what you covered and for how long, or switch to a week for the detail. Your recordings and daily log are kept there too.

FAQ

Questions

Is Revisely free?

Yes. It’s free to download and use, and there are no ads. If you’d like to help keep it that way, you can donate.

Is there an Android version?

It’s coming to Google Play. For now, Revisely is on iPhone.

Which mushaf does it use?

The 604-page Madani mushaf, set in the King Fahd Complex script, with the same page layout as the printed copy many people memorize from.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your plan keeps moving, so missed pages don’t pile up. If you started yesterday’s revision but didn’t finish, Home offers to add the pages you missed to today, and you can always jump to the juz, surah or page you want to start from.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google or email so your progress is saved and syncs between devices. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in Settings.

Does Revisely use my location?

Only if you use prayer times, the Qibla compass or Start day at fajr. Prayer times and the Qibla are worked out on your phone, and your location isn’t sent to us. Start day at fajr saves it to your account, to work out fajr where you are. The Privacy Policy has the details.
