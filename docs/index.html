Today

Know where today starts.

Each day has its portion: today, Al‑Mujadila to At‑Tahrim in Juz 28. Everything else you’ve memorized comes round in turn.

Time and record

Time and record your recitation.

A stopwatch and a recorder on the page. Each sitting is saved to your day with its ayahs and minutes, to listen back later.

Search

Find any ayah in a few words.

Type a few words of an ayah, or say them on iPhone, and go straight to it: here, Ayat al‑Kursi in Al‑Baqarah.

Translations

Read the meaning in your language.

Hold an ayah for its translation. Choose from 76 translations in 56 languages, star the ones you use, and download them to read offline.

Activity

Every day at a glance.

A calendar of what you revised, memorized and read, and the juz you haven’t revised in a while.

Prayer times and the Qibla

Prayer times, and which way to face.

The day’s times for where you are, worked out on your phone, and a Qibla compass beside them.

Offline

Works without a connection.

Download the whole mushaf once and revise anywhere. What you log offline is kept on your phone and syncs when you’re back.

Revo

Ask Revo how your revision is going.

Revo is an optional assistant in Revisely. Ask how your week went or what to revise first, and it answers from your own revision. Ask how something in the app works, and it explains.

  • How was my week?
  • What should I revise first?
  • How do I log revision from the masjid?
  • How did today go?
  • Off until you turn it on. Turning it off in Settings deletes its conversations and what it remembers.
  • It only suggests: nothing in your plan changes unless you tap it.
  • Its answers are written by Claude, from Anthropic. Anthropic doesn’t train its models on what Revo sends.

Revo can make mistakes, so check anything that matters against the mushaf. What Revo sends, and what’s kept
