Support

Questions, feedback or something not working? Email support@revisely.app and you’ll hear back from the developer.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Revisely version (Settings, at the bottom of the list), and a screenshot if you have one.

  • Moving to a new phone

    Install Revisely and sign in with the same Apple, Google or email account. Your memorized pages, plan and history are synced to your account.

  • Not getting the daily reminder

    Check that reminders are on in Revisely’s Settings, under Notifications, and that notifications are allowed for Revisely in the iPhone’s Settings app.

  • Fell behind on your plan

    If you didn’t finish yesterday’s pages, Home offers to add them to today. To shift your whole plan by a day, use the “Behind?” option on Home.

  • Deleting your account

    In Revisely, open Settings and choose Delete account. Your account and all of its data are deleted. The Privacy Policy explains what is kept and for how long.

  • Android

    Revisely is coming to Google Play. For now it is available for iPhone on the App Store.
